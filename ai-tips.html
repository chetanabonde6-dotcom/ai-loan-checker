Financial Quick Tips

💡 Keep your credit utilisation below 30% of your total credit limit.
📅 Set up auto-debit for loan EMIs — one missed payment can drop your score by 50–70 points.
🏦 Compare at least 3 lenders before choosing — a 0.5% rate difference saves lakhs over long tenures.
🔄 Refinancing a high-interest loan after your score improves can save significant interest.
